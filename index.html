Hermes

A private, single-user personal assistant.

Hermes is a personal assistant that runs on its owner's own computer. It is used by one person only and is not offered to the public.

With the owner's permission, Hermes reads the owner's Gmail and Google Calendar to answer the owner's own questions, such as summarising the week ahead or adding up income from payment emails.

Hermes has read-only access. It cannot send, change or delete email or calendar events.
