Hermes Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Who uses Hermes

Hermes is a personal assistant used only by its owner. It is not available to anyone else, and no other person's account is connected to it.

What data Hermes accesses

With the owner's consent through Google sign-in, Hermes requests two read-only permissions:

  • Gmail (read-only): to read and search the owner's email.
  • Google Calendar (read-only): to view the owner's calendar events.

Hermes cannot send, modify or delete email or calendar events.

How the data is used

Data is used only to answer the owner's own requests, for example summarising upcoming events or totalling income from payment emails. It is not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

Where the data goes

Hermes runs on the owner's own computer. Google data is not sold, shared with third parties or published. Depending on the owner's settings, Hermes may send the content needed for a request to the AI model provider the owner has chosen, solely to process that request.

Storage and retention

The Google sign-in token is stored only on the owner's computer. Hermes does not keep a separate copy of email or calendar data beyond what is needed to answer a request.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Hermes's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections.
